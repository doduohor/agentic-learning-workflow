# Mission: Kotlin Backend Production Readiness

## Why
Prepare for Junior+/Middle Kotlin Backend work, especially systems similar to CUP RT / AIS MIDIO, by learning to reason about production backend behavior instead of memorizing isolated definitions.

## Success looks like
- Explain backend trade-offs around transactions, messaging, consistency, reliability, observability, security, and deployment using concrete failure modes.
- Design and defend Kotlin/Ktor service flows using Postgres, RabbitMQ, Docker, metrics, and tests.
- Spot unsafe interview answers and production designs before they become incidents.

## Constraints
- Lessons must stay short and practice-heavy.
- Prefer official documentation and durable sources over tutorials.
- Tie examples to booking, MIDIO-like workflows, and backend interview situations.

## Out of scope
- Frontend development.
- Cloud-provider-specific trivia unless it teaches a durable backend principle.
- Large theoretical surveys without a practical failure scenario.
